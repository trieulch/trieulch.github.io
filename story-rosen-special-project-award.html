Beyond the Limits: A Special Project Award for Our KPI Dashboard

Award · ROSEN · Team lead

Our KPI Tracking Dashboard team received ROSEN’s Special Project Award for Technical Excellence & Operational Impact.

My role

I led the team through the three-month project:

  • Coordinating the work. Planned the team’s tasks and kept the project moving.
  • Working with stakeholders in Lingen, Germany, and in Malaysia.
  • Presenting to senior leadership, making sure the project was clear to them.
  • Preparing every presentation: reviewing the material and running rehearsals before we presented.

The challenges

  • It was outside our scope of work. Senior management asked for the project, but it was not part of our SOW. Everyone still had their full daily workload, so we had to find time for it on top of our regular jobs.
  • Key stakeholders were overseas. The teams in other countries held the licenses and the authority we needed. Approvals moved slowly and took many rounds of meetings. Things only sped up once our Head of Department stepped in.

How we solved them

  • A fixed 30 minutes every day. I set aside a daily block for the team to work on the project. That small, steady amount of time kept the work moving without hurting our daily work.
  • Roles based on strengths. I split the work by what each person did best: one built the semantic model, one designed the visualizations, and one handled QA/QC and helped prepare the pitch.
  • Speaking the stakeholders’ language. Because I understood the technical side of Power BI, it was easy to discuss requirements with the overseas teams and get answers faster.
  • Planned meetings and clear priorities. I scheduled meetings ahead and prioritized what we needed from each stakeholder, so the project did not drag on.
