Ultimate Budget Template

Inspired by The Office Lab

Ultimate financial planning: a personal budget template in Excel with a zero-based plan, actual tracking and an interactive dashboard.

Step by step

  1. Budget Plan Setup. Build a zero-based, multi-period budget plan, including irregular income and expenses (yearly, quarterly or changing month to month).
  2. Budget Tracking Setup. Create a section to record actual income and spending so you can compare it against the plan.
  3. Dashboard: Initial Setup. Lay out the dashboard foundation, including the year and period selectors.
  4. Dashboard: Breakdown Section. Build the Income, Expenses and Savings tables with Tracked, Budget, % Compl., Remaining and Excess columns.
  5. Dashboard: Category Distribution Charts. Add donut charts showing how income, expenses and savings split across categories.
  6. Dashboard: Tracked vs. Budget Chart. Add a monthly column chart comparing tracked amounts with the budget.
  7. Dashboard: Performance KPI Tiles. Add KPI tiles such as Period Completion, Tracking Balance and Savings Rate.
  8. Finishing Touches. Polish the formatting and double-check the whole workbook.
